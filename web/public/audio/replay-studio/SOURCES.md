# Replay studio audio sources

All source recordings in this directory are released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication. Attribution is not required; the source record is retained here for provenance and future remastering.

## Fight recordings

- `foley/face-punches.mp3` — “Face Punch, Body Impact, 4x” by Kinoton: https://freesound.org/s/503277/
- `foley/power-kicks.mp3` — “Power Kick, Body Impact, 7x” by Kinoton: https://freesound.org/s/586841/
- `foley/bag-impacts.mp3` — “Hits & Fight - Punching” by Vrymaa: https://freesound.org/s/753427/
- `foley/air-movement.mp3` — “Hits & Fight - Dodge” by Vrymaa: https://freesound.org/s/753428/
- `foley/blade-whoosh.mp3` — “Whoosh 01” by velcronator: https://freesound.org/s/733888/
- `foley/energy-discharge.mp3` — “Energy Blast” by Kinoton: https://freesound.org/s/369516/
- `foley/electricity.mp3` — “Electricity” by FlashTrauma: https://freesound.org/s/398274/
- `foley/phase-shift.mp3` — “Teleport” by BaggoNotes: https://freesound.org/s/719434/
- `foley/body-fall.mp3` — “Falling body hits the floor” by courtneyeck: https://freesound.org/s/673424/

The Freesound assets are the service-provided high-quality preview encodes of their corresponding CC0 originals.

https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
